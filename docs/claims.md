# Claims

- Mechanism claim tested: a safety filter is novel only if its interventions teach the policy safe-boundary structure that persists after the filter is removed.
- Evidence claim: a deterministic safe-control learning benchmark with CBF, MPC, conformal, recovery, clipping, projection, ablation, and stress comparisons was added.
- Supported local finding: counterfactual boundary learning reduces unshielded post-training violations and filter dependence.
- Unsupported main claim: the method is not submission-ready because it sacrifices deployed task success and deployed safety relative to standard shields.
- Unsupported claim explicitly avoided: no claim of SOTA safe robot learning, real robot deployment, or high-fidelity simulator validation.
